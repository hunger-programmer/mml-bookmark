## Discovery context

mml-bookmark is intended for anyone who saves recipes and ideas online. The initial product concept focuses on saving content from sources such as YouTube/Shorts and recipe websites, with a consistent recipe view. Browser and mobile applications are intended distribution platforms; their exact rollout remains to be decided.
