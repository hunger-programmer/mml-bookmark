# D08 — Pilot scope: links in, recipes stored and browsable; no grocery lists, meal planning or cookbooks
- Date, owner: 2026-10-22, Paweł Wójcik (founder)
- Context and the options weighed: ReciMe offers recipe import plus grocery lists, meal planning and cookbooks. The founder's brief proposed excluding grocery lists and meal planning. Asked whether "reproduce ReciMe functionality" includes them.
- Decision and why: "link in, repice stored in db, browsable recipe out. Do not implement grocery lists, meal or cookbooks, focus only on links and preparing recipe" (founder's words).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: The pilot competes with ReciMe only on import, browsing and categories. Revisit through a superseding decision by the founder.
- Status: active

## Update 2026-10-22 (founder)
- Also excluded for now: nutrition estimation, serving conversion, social features, translation. Founder: "do not implement now nutrition, serving conversion, social features, translation".
