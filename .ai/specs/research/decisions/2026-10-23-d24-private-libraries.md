# D24 — Each user's links are private
- Date, owner: 2026-10-23, Paweł Wójcik (founder)
- Context and the options weighed: R07 ("each user's library is private") was a proposed pilot default.
- Decision and why: Founder: "Stored links should not be visible or achievable by other people."
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Every read of a stored link is scoped to its owner. Replaces R07 with R11.
- Status: active
