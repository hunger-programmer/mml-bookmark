## Decision summary

Paweł Wójcik chose to start the pilot build now (D01) and to build it even if ReciMe covers their needs, because they want a tool that fits them (D10). No extraction system exists yet, so nothing has been tested before building.

The only user story on file is the founder's. They saved a YouTube recipe as a Chrome bookmark and watched the video 7 times while cooking to get the ingredients. A comment held the ingredients and steps, but they found it only after cooking (Problems). Nobody else has been asked. The workaround today is rewatching the video.

What is being built (D08, D27–D44): link in, recipe stored, browsable recipe out.
- Accounts: open sign-up through Supabase with email + password, Google or Apple (D22, D23, D33). Links are private, with one exception: admins can impersonate a user read-only and audit-logged, without disclosure (R20, D51; GDPR risk accepted).
- Capture: the iOS app (Expo, capture only), the Chrome extension, or a pasted URL, built in parallel (D11, D34). Each user gets 5 processed saves a day; overflow is stored and processed the next day; admins are exempt (R19).
- Sorting: at most one of sport exercises, recipe, fun; the rest is Uncategorised (R12). Only `recipe` items get extraction (R14).
- Recipes: description, comments and linked page first, then captions, then yt-dlp + Whisper (D19, D26), with OpenAI as the model (D38). Several recipe cards per bookmark (D31), translated to English (R10), editable, never overwritten by reprocessing (R15). The transcript is shown in its original language (R18).
- Library: the web works fully on a phone (D34); text search covers titles, ingredients and notes (D32). There is also an admin area and one onboarding screen (D42, D43).
- Out: everything else from the earlier concept (N08), plus grocery lists, meal planning, cookbooks, nutrition, serving conversion, social features, interface translation and export (N01, N06, N10).

What could change the plan:
- Whether a usable recipe can be extracted from YouTube without invented facts (R01) is untested (A02). Order is decided: description, comments and linked recipe page first, spoken content as fallback (D19). For spoken content: existing captions through a transcript API, then yt-dlp + Whisper (D26). Both are unofficial and may conflict with YouTube's terms; neither has been verified yet. The founder stops only if no method works at all (D13).
- Whether other people have this problem is untested and will not be tested (A01, D20). The team build for themselves (D18); that is a choice, not evidence about others.
- ReciMe has a browser extension (founder's correction). The reason to build is the founder's own fit, not a shown gap in competitors (D10).

Next: the platform-foundation spec (D53) with the stack and standards changes in D46–D51, then feature specs for extraction, the clients and the library. Then build the extractor alongside the three clients (D11) and run it on the founder's own saved links, including the video rewatched 7 times and its comment (A02). Dates are unset.

## Product and how it stands out

- What it is: a personal library for saving links and reading recipes in one layout, sorted by category automatically (see Scope). `[DOCUMENT]` `.ai/specs/research/2026-10-22-mml-bookmark-brief.md`
- What makes it different: no difference from ReciMe is claimed. The founder first said ReciMe lacked browser support, then corrected it: ReciMe has a browser extension. The founder builds for their own fit (D10). `[INTERVIEW]` `.ai/specs/research/interviews/2026-10-22-founder-recipe-saving.md`
- ReciMe lists a web app and a Chrome extension; its help page says recipe import does not read comments, which the founder's own case relied on. `[BENCHMARK]` `.ai/specs/research/2026-10-22-mml-bookmark-competitive-landscape.md` (Platform support); https://www.recime.app/help/en/articles/14773584-why-didn-t-my-recipe-import-correctly
- Close competitors already exist (Stasht, Sorti for social/recipe saving; Raindrop, mymind for general libraries); the proposition is not a new category. `[BENCHMARK]` `.ai/specs/research/2026-10-22-mml-bookmark-competitive-landscape.md`

## Scope

- **Now:** the pilot build per D01–D44, summarised in the Decision summary and governed by R01–R19. Clients are built in parallel from day one (D11); the earlier recommended sequence (extraction test first) is not adopted. ## Key flows

- Current state: the founder bookmarks the YouTube link in Chrome, cooks from the video and rewatches it 7 times to get the ingredients. `[INTERVIEW]` `.ai/specs/research/interviews/2026-10-22-founder-recipe-saving.md`

- Future state, step 2: background processing assigns at most one category (R12); items in `recipe` get recipe cards, partial recipes or the link-and-preview fallback (R02, R03, R14), with automatic retries and then a Try again button (R17). `[DOCUMENT]` `.ai/specs/research/decisions/2026-10-23-d27-d44-grilling-session.md`
- Future state, step 3: on a phone or desktop browser, the user browses by category or Uncategorised or searches titles, ingredients and notes, then opens the bookmark: its recipe cards, note, transcript and original source. `[DOCUMENT]` `.ai/specs/research/decisions/2026-10-23-d27-d44-grilling-session.md`
